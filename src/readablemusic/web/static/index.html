ReadableMusic

Turn a scan or PDF of a cappella sheet music into a clean MusicXML file and PDF. You can fix the lyrics in your browser before downloading.

  • Printed music only. Lyrics in English for now.
  • Scan straight, at 300 DPI if you can. Phone photos work best flat and evenly lit.
  • Processing takes about a minute per page. Results are deleted after 24 hours.

Free and open source (AGPL-3.0): source code. Recognition by Audiveris, engraving by Verovio.
