ReadableMusic

Turn a scan or PDF of a cappella sheet music into a clean MusicXML file and PDF. You can fix the lyrics in your browser before downloading.

One photo for each page, in order.

    • Printed music only. Lyrics in English for now.
    • Scan straight, at 300 DPI if you can. To photograph a page, lay it flat, hold the phone straight above it so that the whole page fills the picture, and avoid shadows.
    • Processing takes about a minute per page, 30 pages at most. Results are deleted after 24 hours.
    • Upload only music you have the right to copy.

    Free and open source (AGPL-3.0): source code. Recognition by Audiveris, engraving by Verovio. Report a problem.
